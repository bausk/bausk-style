# Archive

Candidate looks that were not chosen (0.1.0, 2026-10-29). They are not built and not available to
projects. They stay here as worked material: to bring one back, move its folder to `tokens/looks/`
and its palette to `tokens/palettes/`, give it status `candidate`, rebuild and run the tests.

- `sheet` · Аркуш: engineering drawing sheet (graphite, title block, red pencil), palette `graphite`.
- `survey` · Планшет: topographic survey sheet / drone ground-station display, palette `survey`.
