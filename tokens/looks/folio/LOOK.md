## Overview

The bausk.dev Ghost theme v1.0 (2026-10-12): a quiet academic page with serif reading text, sans metadata, hairlines and one brick-red accent. It is kept frozen so the existing theme can adopt the system without a visual change. Do not use it for new work; it sits in the most common AI-generated look of 2025–26 (warm cream paper, serif, terracotta accent, hairlines).

## Colors

Warm paper `#faf9f6`, warm ink in three steps, hairline `#d9d5cc`, brick accent `#8f2a1a`. The dark scheme was derived for completeness and never shipped on the site.

## Typography

Source Serif 4 (optical sizes) for reading and titles, Source Sans 3 for interface and metadata, Source Code Pro for code. Body 19px at 1.62 leading on a 41rem column.

## Layout

Reading column 41rem, wide figures 62rem, page frame 74rem; contents list in the left margin on wide screens.

## Elevation & Depth

None.

## Shapes

Square corners; 1px hairlines and a 2px ink rule over section heads.

## Components

Shared components with no signature changes.

## Do's and Don'ts

- **Don't** start new projects in this look; choose the brand's default look.
- **Do** keep it unchanged; it is a record of what shipped.
