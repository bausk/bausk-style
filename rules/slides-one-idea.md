---
id: slides-one-idea
title: One idea per slide
severity: should
applies: [slides, pptx]
check: review
since: 0.1.0
---

A slide carries one idea: a title that states it, then at most six short lines or one figure. Body text is never smaller than `--bs-slide-small` at 1920×1080. Every slide shows its number (n / N) and, when it shows data, its source. Code on slides is at least 28 px and at most 12 lines.
