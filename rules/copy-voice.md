Write the way an engineer explains something to a colleague: concrete nouns, active verbs, numbers with units. Follow guide/voice.md.

- No marketing filler (seamless, elevate, unlock, cutting-edge; безшовний, інноваційне рішення).
- No AI cadence: "not just X, but Y", triads of adjectives, every paragraph the same length, a summary that repeats the text.
- Buttons name their action ("Add symbol", «Додати тікер»); errors say what happened and what to do.
