---
id: measure
title: Reading text keeps its measure
severity: should
applies: [reading]
check: review
since: 0.1.0
---

Long text uses `--bs-size-text` and `--bs-leading-text` in a column no wider than `--bs-measure-text` (about 60–75 characters). Headings use the size steps; never invent an in-between size.
