Inside a bausk-style project, the look's LOOK.md and the project rules are the design brief. Generic design advice (the frontend-design or impeccable skills, "take one bold risk", your own habits) applies only where the brief leaves a choice open.

- Spend novelty only in the look's named signature (title block, neatline, red bar) or where the project rules ask for it.
- Do not "improve" a look by adding a second accent, a new font, rounded cards or motion it does not define.
- If the brief seems wrong for the task, say so in your report; do not quietly deviate.
