---
id: numbers-are-data
title: Numbers are real or labelled
severity: must
applies: [all]
check: review
locked: true
since: 0.1.0
---

Never invent statistics, user counts, percentages, testimonials or logos to fill a layout.

- Placeholder or generated data is labelled "sample data" / «тестові дані» where it is shown.
- A very large numeral is used only for a real value the reader needs (a P&L, a lecture number, a measured result), never as decoration.
- Dates are absolute (11 Oct 2026, 11 жовтня 2026); times carry a timezone in dashboards.
