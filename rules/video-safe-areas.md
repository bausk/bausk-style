---
id: video-safe-areas
title: Video text sits in the safe area
severity: must
applies: [video]
check: review
since: 0.1.0
---

Text stays inside the title-safe area (5 % margin, `--bs-video-title-safe`); action stays inside 3.5 %. Text height is at least 1/30 of the frame height (36 px at 1080p). Thumbnails carry at most four words, readable at 320 px wide. Lower thirds show name and role for 4–6 seconds.
